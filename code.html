Software & Algorithmic Composition Systems

PianoML (13-EDO)

Ali Balighi • Machine Learning (FluCoMa, SuperCollider)
A generative piano work in which models trained on a corpus of Chopin MIDI performances compose in 13-EDO, a tuning no piano repertoire was written for.
Code: https://github.com/alib65/PianoML-13EDO

MultiCue

Ali Balighi • C++
A free, open-source cue-based multichannel audio and video show player for theatre, concerts, installations, and electroacoustic music. Supports 4–64+ channel speaker feeds and spatial audio output (VBAP, Ambisonics/AllRAD, binaural SOFA HRTFs, stereo downmix, B-format).
Code: https://github.com/alib65/MultiCue

Multichannel Player

Ali Balighi • SuperCollider
A SuperCollider playlist player for multichannel music from mono files, with 4, 8, 16, 18 or more channels. Builds playlists, remembers track levels, tests speakers, and auto-levels every piece to equal loudness, listened to in direct multichannel, 3D Ambisonics, VBAP, binaural, stereo, or B-format.
Code: https://github.com/alib65/MultichannelPlayer

Speaker Loudness Tester

Ali Balighi • SuperCollider
A 4-channel speaker loudness tester for SuperCollider. Plays pink noise, white noise, sine tones, or sweeps on each speaker separately or together, with level meters, per-channel trims, auto-cycle, and optional mic-based auto-matching.
Code: https://github.com/alib65/SPEAKER-LOUDNESS-TESTER

Mad-X

Ali Balighi • Electronic Musical Device
A DIY MIDI controller built from scratch, an electronic musical instrument designed and constructed from the ground up.
Code: https://github.com/alib65/Mad-X

pianoGPT (v18, compound) — Unpublished

Ali Balighi • PyTorch
pianoGPT is a symbolic Transformer training pipeline for composing complete solo-piano MIDI pieces from the MIDI files in MIDI-BU. It is not an OpenAI custom GPT and it does not fine-tune a text model: it trains a local symbolic-music model from scratch with PyTorch.
