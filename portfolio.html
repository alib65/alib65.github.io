Selected Projects & Media

MultiCue

Ali Balighi • C++ • GPL-3.0
MultiCue is a cue-based, multichannel audio and video show player for theatre, concerts, installations and electroacoustic music, with support for 4–64+ channel speaker feeds and spatial audio output (VBAP, Ambisonics/AllRAD, binaural SOFA HRTFs, stereo downmix, B-format). Free, GPL-3.0.
Code: https://github.com/alib65/MultiCue

Multichannel Player and Speaker Loudness Tester

Ali Balighi • SuperCollider
Multichannel Player is a SuperCollider app for playing multichannel music from mono files, with 4, 8, 16, 18 or more channels. Build playlists, set and remember track levels, test speakers, and auto-level every piece to equal loudness. Listen in direct multichannel, 3D Ambisonics, VBAP, binaural, stereo or B-format.
Code: https://github.com/alib65/MultichannelPlayer

Electropoem No. 12

Ali Balighi • Fixed Media, 4-channel
The Electropoem series is a collection of multichannel compositions that explore the connections between sound, space, and poetic expression. Each piece focuses on the spatial placement and movement of sound to create an engaging listening experience where the balance between sound and silence encourages attentive listening.
Electropoem No. 12 for 4-channel by Ali Balighi

Mad-X

Ali Balighi • Electronic Musical Device
Eight-channel, proximity-based digital musical instrument; eight Adafruit VCNL4030 sensors read hand distance, and SuperCollider defines tuning, gesture-to-sound mapping, synthesis, articulation, timbre, and spatial behavior (article in d.a.t., October 2026).
Code: https://github.com/alib65/Mad-X

Void

Ali Balighi • SuperCollider
A node-based experimental music instrument for SuperCollider, featuring microtonal tuning, MIDI, granular synthesis, sequencers, effects, patch saving, and 50 playable studies.
Code: https://github.com/alib65/Void

MicroMS-01

Ali Balighi • SuperCollider
MicroMS-01 is a modular synthesizer built in SuperCollider with virtual patch cables, 81 module designs, 52 presets, 7–120 EDO tuning, and 4–32-channel spatial audio with AmbiX, stereo and binaural preview.
Code: https://github.com/alib65/MicroMS-01

The Wind Will Carry Us Away

Ali Balighi • Strings and Fixed Media
"The Wind Will Carry Us Away" is a new work for string orchestra and fixed media, selected as one of three winning compositions from the MIT Special Call for Scores: Works for String Orchestra and Electronics, presented in partnership with ICMC Boston 2025. Out of 46 international submissions, this piece was chosen for its unique sonic language and compelling fusion of acoustic and electronic textures. Drawing inspiration from poetic imagery and human fragility in the face of time, the work reflects a nuanced narrative shaped by technology, memory, and motion.

Dastgah: Algorithmic Explorations of Persian Microtonality and Serialism

Ali Balighi • Algorithmic Composition
"Dastgah" (2024) synthesizes Persian theory and Western serialism via SuperCollider. Utilizing a 17-tone microtonal matrix inspired by Dastgah modes, it reimagines serial structures to produce an evolving, cross-cultural soundscape that explores complex pitch relationships and musical identity.
