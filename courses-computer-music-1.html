Computer Music I

MUCP 4341-001 • Fall 2026

An introductory survey of digital sound production, synthesis, and composition. The course covers audio fundamentals, digital audio workstations, sound synthesis (subtractive, additive, FM, granular), real-time music programming in SuperCollider and Max/MSP, and hardware/software integration, culminating in an original final composition or interactive sound project.
